Gravitational Instantons & Riemannian Black Holes

In preparation

A planned essay surveying gravitational instantons — Ricci-flat and Einstein four-manifolds — and their relation to Euclidean black-hole geometries. Topics likely to be discussed include Taub–NUT, Eguchi–Hanson, the AdS-Schwarzschild instanton, asymptotically locally Euclidean and ALF geometries, and the role of these solutions in Euclidean quantum gravity, gravitational thermodynamics, and uniqueness questions for Einstein metrics.
