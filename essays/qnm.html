Quasinormal Modes of Black Holes

Supervised by Prof. Harvey Reall  ·  University of Cambridge

This essay treats the quasinormal-mode (QNM) problem for black holes from a geometric and operator-theoretic perspective: the QNM spectrum as the non-self-adjoint spectrum of a wave operator on a black-hole background, the analytic structure of the resolvent, and the implications for ringdown phenomenology and recent work on QNM completeness in asymptotically flat and AdS spacetimes.
