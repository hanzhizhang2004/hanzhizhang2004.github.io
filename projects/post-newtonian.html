Improved Post-Newtonian Waveforms for Precessing, Eccentric Compact Binaries with Tides

University of Birmingham  ·  Prof. Patricia Schmidt

This project builds on a recently developed post-Newtonian waveform model that already incorporates spin precession and orbital eccentricity, and extends it to include adiabatic tidal effects coupled to eccentricity. The motivation is to study the impact of tides on eccentric binary neutron-star inspirals — a regime that is increasingly relevant for current and next-generation gravitational-wave detectors, and one where the interplay between eccentricity, precession, and tidal deformability is not fully understood.

Scope (preliminary)

  • Review of the existing eccentric, precessing PN waveform model.
  • Inclusion of adiabatic tides coupled to eccentricity, drawing on recent results (e.g. 2403.07043 and related).
  • Validation against existing waveform models and equation-of-state-aware comparisons for binary neutron-star inspirals.
  • Possible parameter-estimation studies to quantify the bias and information loss from neglecting eccentric-tidal couplings.
