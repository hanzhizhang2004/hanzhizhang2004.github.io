Self-Force and Gauge Dependence in Black-Hole Scattering

University of Southampton  ·  Prof. Leor Barack

This project sits inside the broader programme of self-force theory for black-hole scattering, and concerns the gauge- and frame-dependence of the black-hole perturbative treatment. It builds directly on Sec. V.C of Barack & Long (2019), where the analysis was carried out for a specific class of orbits; the goal here is to extend those calculations to general scattering orbits.

Scope (preliminary)

  • Review of black-hole perturbation theory in different gauges, with particular attention to how observables depend on gauge/frame choices.
  • Extension of the calculation in Barack 2019, §V.C, to generic unbound (scattering) geodesics.
  • Coordination with Dr. Kevin Cunningham's ongoing work on a larger scattering self-force programme.

Status

Project planned for 2026. Notes, derivations, and outputs will appear here as the work develops.
