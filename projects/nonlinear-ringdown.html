Nonlinear Ringdown

Tsinghua University  ·  Prof. Huan Yang

A planned project on nonlinear ringdown — the late-time, post-merger emission of a binary black hole, in the regime where second-order (and higher) effects in the perturbation theory of the remnant Kerr spacetime become important. The precise topic is to be defined.

Possible directions

  • Second-order quasinormal modes and their amplitudes as observables in numerical-relativity waveforms.
  • Detectability of nonlinear modes with current and next-generation gravitational-wave observatories.
  • Connections to recent work on QNM completeness and nonlinear perturbation theory.

Status

Topic and precise scope to be defined. Updates will be added here.
