Research Experience

Gravitational-Wave Searches using Pulsar Timing Arrays 06.2025 – 09.2025
Max Planck Institute for Gravitational Physics (AEI) — Dr. Rutger van Haasteren
  • A project extending the calculation of $p$-values for quadratic statistics in PTAs to the Fourier domain.
  • Derived the PTA detection statistic in the Fourier domain and performed dimension reduction of its quadratic filter matrix.
  • Evaluated posterior-predictive $p$-values via MCMC through generalised $\chi^2$ distributions.

Summer Research: Diamagnetic Levitation 07.2024 – 09.2024
University College London — Prof. Peter Barker
  • A computational-physics project on levitating a diamagnetic body above a permanent magnet.
  • Developed a numerical method to compute the optimal shape of a diamagnetic body maximising the levitation height above a rotationally symmetric permanent magnet (tested with a uniformly magnetised cylindrical core with an oppositely magnetised cylindrical shell).
  • Analysed the stability and dynamical properties of the levitated body, and numerically computed the optimal shape for maximum horizontal oscillation frequency.
Independent Project: Multi-Body Problem and Lagrangian Points 04.2024

  • Analysed the existence and stability of the five Lagrangian points.
